Thank you for helping to make the Techspressionism Video Archive more accurate. The transcripts are made by computer and contain errors, especially in names, art terms and technical words. Every suggestion is read by the archive's editor before anything changes.

  1. Play the moment (the link you came from starts at that passage).
  2. Correct the words in the box below. Keep the speaker's own words: only “uh” and “um” are removed, and “like”, “you know” and repeated words stay as spoken. Please do not tidy grammar.
  3. Spell names, artworks and products correctly. If you cannot tell what was said, leave it and say so in the note.
  4. Do not add email addresses, phone numbers or private details, even if a speaker said them aloud.
