Salon 110, "Loop Art Critique" (September 3, 2026), is moderated by Tommy Mintz and based on a collaboration between the Techspressionist community and Loop Art Critique, a virtual community and space. Colin Goldberg [[0:02|introduces the collaboration and notes that live translation captions are available]]. Ariel Baron-Robbins, a founder of Loop, [[2:29|describes its history, philosophy and purpose]], including [[13:10|keeping one person's philosophy of art from limiting who takes part]]. Carolina Kleine Samson [[25:31|talks about her role as graphic designer for the site]], and Sahar Moussavi [[40:11|describes a six-week Loop residency]] and [[53:41|a piece she made there]]. Renata Janiszewska [[58:30|shows a space she created, inspired by Louise Bourgeois]]. The salon ends with audience questions, including [[1:25:38|access for people with slower connections]].
