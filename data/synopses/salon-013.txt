Salon 13 (March 16, 2021), moderated by Davonte Bradley, takes Women's History Month as its theme. Negin Ehtesabian [[5:30|presents from Tehran]] and describes [[14:14|processing everything in digital form]]. Diane Marsella [[25:30|presents next]], and Sue Beyer [[43:15|introduces herself as having "found her people"]] and [[51:33|describes her video work shown on phones, iPads and LED matrices]]. In the discussion afterward, the group talks about [[1:03:25|the excitement of the early NFT scene]], [[1:20:10|Twitter as the place where collectors are]] and [[1:28:16|an NFT platform for artists]].
