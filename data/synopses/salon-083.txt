Salon 83, "Influences" (June 6, 2024), is hosted by Colin Goldberg, who [[12:19|explains that Patrick Lichty, who was to moderate, could not attend]]. Artists talk about what has influenced them: [[30:43|Verneda Lights, who shows a cat on a windowsill among other images]], [[43:52|Karen LaFleur, on a project she has worked on since the start of the pandemic]] and [[49:44|Roz Dimon, on John Berger]]. The group discusses [[1:03:10|animation as an overlooked medium]] and [[1:10:35|sourcing in the age of AI, likened to the Gutenberg press of imagery]], and Goldberg [[1:13:11|comments on the monthly format]].
