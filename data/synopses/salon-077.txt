Salon 77 (December 7, 2023), moderated by Colin Goldberg, is on the topic of sculpture and is much longer than the usual salon. Steve Miller [[7:26|presents a project at Longhouse in East Hampton]], and Goldberg [[29:22|introduces Suzanne Anker, a pioneer in bio art]]. Anker discusses [[42:43|early microscopes and glass]] and [[1:04:39|how diversity of knowledge production can address long-standing problems]]. Karen LaFleur and Jan Swinburne also present. The later part of the recording is a hands-on session in which Michael Pierre Price explains [[1:55:48|how to place artwork in a 3D gallery space using rotation values]], [[2:27:15|why to save often]] and [[2:37:04|what artists need to provide for the launch of the virtual gallery]].
