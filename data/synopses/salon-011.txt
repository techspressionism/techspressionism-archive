Salon 11 (February 16, 2021), moderated by Davonte Bradley, features five artists. Steve Miller [[1:20|opens the session]]. Ryan Henderson [[19:00|presents his work built from music]], in which [[27:17|each slice is one second of a song]]. Lauren Woods [[32:23|presents as an artist and writer]], Jan Swinburne [[43:30|gives an overview of her practice]] and [[1:03:37|talks about carving by hand during the pandemic]], and Lucie Králíková [[1:08:26|shows her latest work]], including [[1:17:06|3D bioprinting]]. The final discussion turns to [[1:50:29|planning a group project with sixteen participants]].
