Salon 54, "Open Studios" (October 12, 2022), is moderated by Davonte Bradley, with artists showing work in progress. Michael Pierre Price [[10:03|describes new work tied to a book he is writing]], Verneda Lights [[15:33|presents from Port Royal, South Carolina]], and Lucy Boyd-Wilson [[20:39|reports on a dome film festival in Anaheim]] and [[24:39|a VR and dome piece]].
