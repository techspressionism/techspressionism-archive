Salon 97, "Creative Coding" (August 7, 2025), features artists who make work with code. Anton Dubrovin [[2:28|presents his work]]. Allen Hirsh, a biophysicist, [[14:34|gives an overview of his system]], showing [[27:43|videos made from a single original photograph]] and [[40:58|an idea that emerged from his particle system]]. Chris Bly, from Portland, Oregon, [[45:56|is introduced through his plotter work]] and shows [[58:59|plotting with pens]] and [[1:12:04|the range of materials that can be plotted on]]. The salon ends with a comparison to [[1:25:34|a candid photographer who goes out not knowing what they will see]].
