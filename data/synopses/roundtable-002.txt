Roundtable 2, "Approach," is moderated by Michael Pierre Price with artists Roz Dimon, Tommy Mintz and Renata Janiszewska. The conversation asks what an artistic approach means when technology is the tool. Participants present work and process, including [[6:01|layers as a way of seeing]], [[12:48|animations they want to make longer]] and [[15:15|custom-made digital brushes]], and discuss [[20:48|using AI to create a preconceived aesthetic]]. Dimon presents [[35:44|a story-driven talk on bringing touch to the digital age]]. Price frames [[49:08|approach as exploration and experimentation]], and the group considers [[53:59|why Colin Goldberg stresses approach over style]], [[54:55|a faster and more informational universe]] and [[1:01:29|the new visual language that is arising]].
