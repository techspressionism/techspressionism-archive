Salon 72, "Environment" (July 6, 2023), is the first of the monthly salons, co-moderated by Lee Schnaiberg and Jaime Bautista. Renata Janiszewska [[29:50|welcomes new attendees, including Moritz Albrecht from Germany]]. Artists present work on the environment: [[12:11|Sahar Moussavi]], [[24:00|Ann Shapiro]], Lucy Boyd-Wilson [[36:04|who grew up in the English countryside]], [[44:46|Bautista]], [[53:07|Susan Detroy]], [[59:20|Yuge Zhou]], Verneda Lights [[1:06:21|with three works]] and [[1:23:55|Stephen Paré]]. The group also discusses [[1:18:57|a trial of a new meeting arrangement]].
