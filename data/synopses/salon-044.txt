Salon 44, "East End Tech" (May 25, 2022), features artists from the Long Island east end who are in the Southampton exhibition. Speakers include Roz Dimon [[7:20|on her drawing "Kalashnikov II"]], Frank Gillette [[13:27|on the nine images he has in the show]], Roy Nicholson [[23:12|on early work made on a Paintbox system, and on a 72-panel commission for waiting rooms]], Christine Sciulli [[35:14|on a line-based piece]], Dan Welden [[44:03|on a steel-backed polymer plate]], [[49:16|Nina Yankowitz]], [[58:59|John Zieman]] and Steve Miller [[1:05:35|who shows a 1993 photograph of Colin Goldberg and reflects on the history of Techspressionism]]. In the closing discussion, [[1:20:21|Miller describes how abstract expressionism and pop shaped his generation]], and [[1:23:05|Gillette responds on text in images]].
