Salon 44, "East End Tech" (May 25, 2022), features artists from the Long Island east end who are in the Southampton exhibition. Speakers include [[7:20|Roz Dimon, on her drawing "Kalashnikov II"]], [[13:27|Frank Gillette, on the nine images he has in the show]], [[23:12|Roy Nicholson, on early work made on a Paintbox system, and on a 72-panel commission for waiting rooms]], [[35:14|Christine Sciulli, on a line-based piece]], [[44:03|Dan Welden, on a steel-backed polymer plate]], [[49:16|Nina Yankowitz]], [[58:59|John Zieman]] and [[1:05:35|Steve Miller, who shows a 1993 photograph of Colin Goldberg and reflects on the history of Techspressionism]]. In the closing discussion, [[1:20:21|Miller describes how abstract expressionism and pop shaped his generation]], and [[1:23:05|Gillette responds on text in images]].
