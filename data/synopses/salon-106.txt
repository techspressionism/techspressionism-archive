Salon 106, "France" (May 7, 2026), is co-hosted with Stephen Paré and features artists from France. Isabelle Arvers [[6:52|describes seven years of mixing ancestral knowledge and computer games, including 3D scans of baobab trees in Senegal]]. Mâa Berriet [[15:51|describes moving from coding to art after first touching computers in 1977]] and [[19:18|an interactive game made for Nuit Blanche in Paris]]. Everardo Reyes [[25:30|presents his work]], including [[39:28|a poem about growing slowly and listening before naming]], and Treeskulltown [[47:34|presents a collection built on the "sacred of simplicity"]]. The discussion covers [[1:01:19|how becoming part of an animated film feels like becoming a hero]] and [[1:14:20|the joy of being selective]].
