In Interview 20, Michael Pierre Price interviews Canadian artist Renata Janiszewska. She recalls [[4:53|Sunday visits to the Art Gallery of Ontario and the Inuit art she saw there]], [[10:02|the effect of her Hungarian mother's wartime experience]] and [[24:09|how digital collage led her to animation]], and shows [[29:25|an animated room]] and work she imagines on [[31:48|a large backlit flat screen in a dark room]]. Price connects her images to [[35:28|the mystery of Magritte]] and [[41:46|a dreamlike psychological quality]], and she explains [[47:53|how she composes with a lot of open space]]. They talk about [[15:26|why artists like meeting others who work in a similar way]], [[20:14|her first collaboration for the third collab show]], [[51:18|her work in two online pavilions]] and [[57:05|how arts funding differs in Canada]].
