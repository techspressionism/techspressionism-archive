Salon 50, "Output & Fabrication" (August 17, 2022), is moderated by Cynthia Beth Rubin and is the group's fiftieth salon. Artists show how they turn digital work into physical objects: Roz Dimon [[17:03|on bringing touch to digital work]], Verneda Lights [[26:06|on her Gullah Geechee collection and output options]], Renata Janiszewska [[33:49|on metallic photographic paper and other materials]], Reese Schroeder [[39:23|on art rugs, fine bone china and architectural art glass]], Gregory Little [[49:19|on prints and a nine-channel video projection]], Susan Detroy [[1:04:38|on canvas prints]] and Patrick Lichty [[1:11:38|on a show with Negin Ehtesabian]]. Sasha Pyle [[55:33|comments on the presentations]] and [[1:24:38|shares her experience with canvas wraps]]. Colin Goldberg [[1:20:31|makes an announcement about what follows the Southampton show]] and [[1:29:12|thanks the presenters]].
