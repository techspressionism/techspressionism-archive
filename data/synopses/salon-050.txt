Salon 50, "Output & Fabrication" (August 17, 2022), is moderated by Cynthia Beth Rubin and is the group's fiftieth salon. Artists show how they turn digital work into physical objects: [[17:03|Roz Dimon, on bringing touch to digital work]], [[26:06|Verneda Lights, on her Gullah Geechee collection and output options]], [[33:49|Renata Janiszewska, on metallic photographic paper and other materials]], [[39:23|Reese Schroeder, on art rugs, fine bone china and architectural art glass]], [[49:19|Gregory Little, on prints and a nine-channel video projection]], [[1:04:38|Susan Detroy, on canvas prints]] and [[1:11:38|Patrick Lichty, on a show with Negin Ehtesabian]]. Sasha Pyle [[55:33|comments on the presentations]] and [[1:24:38|shares her experience with canvas wraps]]. Colin Goldberg [[1:20:31|makes an announcement about what follows the Southampton show]] and [[1:29:12|thanks the presenters]].
