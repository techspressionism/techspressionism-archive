Roundtable 3, "Science & Art: Part One," is moderated by Michael Pierre Price with artists Allen Hirsh, Karen LaFleur and Cynthia Beth Rubin, who are collaborating with scientists through a University of Rhode Island program. LaFleur and Rubin share their projects, starting with [[8:58|LaFleur's screen-share of her project]] and [[14:43|Rubin's presentation]], and the group discusses [[26:25|how the collaboration has affected them as artists]]. Hirsh says [[27:52|it pushed him deeper into animation]] and describes [[32:58|the "aha" moment of building a narrative from deep-sea imagery]], while LaFleur describes [[31:14|learning new software]]. They recall [[38:34|the meeting where scientists and artists were brought together]] and [[50:37|the scientists from Woods Hole who signed up]], consider [[44:36|how the work stretched their creativity]] and [[48:04|why the marriage of art and science matters for the planet]], and Rubin offers [[49:28|a dissenting view about artists and aesthetics]]. LaFleur ends with [[54:07|an animation from the project]].
