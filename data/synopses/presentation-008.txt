Presentation 8, "Digital Sculpture and World Creation," is the final session of the American Artist Talks series with the American MakerSpace in Qarshi, Uzbekistan, moderated by Michael Pierre Price. Lucy Boyd-Wilson explains [[6:05|why she uses Unity for interactive art]] and presents [[9:20|"Bioluminescent Cave," a virtual-reality world built in Tilt Brush and finished in Unity]]. Annette Weintraub presents [[15:31|her cylindrical panoramas, from "Sampling Broadway" in 1999]], [[18:36|the vinyl version shown in the CAMUZ exhibition]] and [[21:42|coming changes to Cinema 4D]]. Two Minecraft builders from Qarshi then show [[24:51|a city they built, with a museum, a stadium and night lighting]]. In the discussion, the presenters talk about [[31:13|3D as another way of seeing the environment]], [[34:39|learning the tools by starting simple]], [[37:57|games with creative interfaces such as Minecraft]] and [[41:36|where ideas for a world come from]]. They also discuss [[47:49|making 3D models from a few photographs]], [[54:18|building a shared space]] and [[57:28|Brutalist architecture in Qarshi and Tashkent]], and Price ends with [[1:00:40|thanks to the hosts and the plan for a page of all the presentations]].
