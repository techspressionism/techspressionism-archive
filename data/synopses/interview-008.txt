In Interview 8, Roz Dimon interviews artist and designer Steve Miller, a co-founder of the Techspressionist group. He remembers [[4:03|wandering the Albright-Knox museum as a child]], [[9:48|abandoning plans to be an architect]] and [[14:44|working digitally as early as 1983]], when video games and primitive cultures collided in his imagery. He explains [[21:19|what he learned at CERN]] and [[27:57|at Brookhaven National Laboratory]] about particles and information, and why [[31:22|information, not pictures, is the product]] in the NFT conversation, including [[40:24|ownership as a number on a ledger]] and [[43:22|artists putting work out without a dealer]]. He shows environmental works, among them [[45:39|a piece made with technology to see the natural world]] and [[48:26|silkscreens on Al Gore's Earth in the Balance]], and describes [[51:30|a museum building at Michigan State University]]. He ends on [[53:12|what Techspressionism means to him]] and [[56:36|the more democratic access to creative space it reflects]].
