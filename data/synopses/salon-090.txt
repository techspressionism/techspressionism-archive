Salon 90, "Origins Part One" (January 2, 2025), is moderated by Michael Pierre Price, with artists looking back at how they came to technology. Steve Miller [[2:33|presents first]], showing [[15:51|images photographed off a screen to show the raster lines]]. Nina Sobell [[48:21|speaks from a personal point of view about her first use of technology]], including [[1:01:22|a project with cameras pointed at the World Trade Center towers]] and [[1:14:36|photographs of early robots]]. Darcy Gerbarg, Lee Musgrave and Adrienne Wortzel also take part.
