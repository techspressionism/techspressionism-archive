Roundtable 4, "Science & Art: Part Two," is moderated by Michael Pierre Price with artist Steve Miller and Moritz Albrecht of Germany. Miller opens with [[1:26|a presentation of his work]], and Albrecht follows with [[30:13|his view that creativity is the fuel and motor of both science and art]]. The discussion covers Miller's [[33:33|painting based on the universal barcode]], [[37:55|the need for resources in the favelas of Rio]] and [[45:38|beauty in science, drawing on a physicist's essay in Surfing the Cosmos]]. They talk about [[43:21|how art and science can compete]], [[49:58|how science brings new content to art]] and [[50:17|the curiosity scientists and artists share]], and Albrecht shows [[55:29|a large printed piece]].
