In Interview 15, Roz Dimon interviews painter and virtual-world builder Darcy Gerbarg. Gerbarg recalls [[4:56|early paintings of the Catskill Mountains]], [[6:02|her cultural influences]] and [[7:58|copying paintings at the Met]], and says she [[11:12|decided to make art whether she could or not]]. She shows [[17:15|a series of 150 small-scale pieces]] and [[19:36|large paintings]], and describes [[27:40|showing virtual worlds with headsets in Miami]], [[32:21|inviting people to visit her worlds]] and [[35:04|exploring new kinds of interaction with art in virtual environments]]. Dimon explains [[33:50|how the Techspressionism salons work]], and Gerbarg recalls [[45:51|a museum show funded by IBM]].
