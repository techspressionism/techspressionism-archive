In Interview 18, Roz Dimon interviews painter and digital artist Randi Matushevitz. Matushevitz traces [[6:34|her move from drawing electives to printmaking]] and shows her strange, striking portraits, made with [[14:12|stencils from her printmaking background]] and at [[15:47|large scale]]. She talks about [[20:20|the influence of big painted faces]], [[24:20|the value of drawing from the figure]] and [[26:09|learning to paint solvent-free]], and explains [[28:56|how her Headspace paintings led to Headspace 3D]]. Dimon responds to [[32:48|their eerie beauty]] and [[35:22|their ambiguity of gender]], and the two discuss [[45:22|spiritual seeking]], [[48:23|Matushevitz's reaction on first hearing the definition of Techspressionism]] and [[53:57|a new visual vocabulary in which everyone is an artist]].
