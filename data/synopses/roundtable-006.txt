Roundtable 6, "Founders: Five Years Later," is moderated by Michael Pierre Price. It brings together five of the people who started the group, Colin Goldberg, Steve Miller, Oz Van Rosen, Patrick Lichty and Helen A. Harrison, to look back on its origins. Goldberg explains [[4:17|how he came up with the term]], Van Rosen describes [[9:27|coming to art after a career in the corporate world]], and Miller recalls [[14:19|working with Goldberg in the studio in the early 1990s]], before the word existed. Harrison argues that [[28:33|technology is both a way of making art and a subject for it]], and Lichty reminds the group that [[38:03|digital art is also material culture]]. They discuss [[35:53|the shift from online meetings to a physical exhibition]], [[45:50|a drone piece]], [[50:47|what happened at the first salon]] and [[59:29|whether Warhol is a useful comparison]]. Miller looks ahead to [[1:01:56|where the group might be in a year]], and Harrison closes with [[1:12:08|a reflection on what none of them could have predicted five years ago]].
