Salon 91, "Origins Part Two" (February 6, 2025), is moderated by Michael Pierre Price on the origins of technology and art. Roz Dimon and Cynthia Beth Rubin present, with Rubin showing [[13:04|a recent drawing about what connects us]]. Carter Hodgkin [[26:09|describes his interest in things moving and extracting from nature]], and Victor Acevedo [[37:11|presents his history]], including [[50:17|the LA Graph community and its newsletters]] and [[1:03:29|early exhibitions]]. Annette Weintraub also presents, and Price [[1:06:20|closes, explaining that he did not begin as an artist working with technology]]. The group then reflects on [[1:19:47|how the conversation has developed over the salons]].
