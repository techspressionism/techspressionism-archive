Salon 26 (September 14, 2021) is the opening reception of Collab #2, the second Techspressionist virtual collaboration exhibition, with Davonte Bradley walking through the paired works. The pairs are Darcy Gerbarg and Michael Pointer, who [[7:02|combine her virtual-world "light sculptures" with his photographs of a house]]; Malavika Mandal Andrew and Ryan Henderson, who [[18:57|make work from her songs and photographs and his sound algorithms]]; Michael Pierre Price and his partner, who [[35:26|create a triptych about religion and science]]; Pointer and Caro Ramonde, who [[54:12|merge an oil painting with a photograph in "Serpent of Creation"]]; Negin Ehtesabian and Brandon Gellis, who [[1:06:12|work on environmental themes]]; Frédéric Pons and Diane Marsella, who [[1:19:55|explore the solar system]]; and Deann Stein Hasinoff and Tommy Mintz, who [[1:30:06|swap and layer images]]. Colin Goldberg [[1:44:16|thanks the organizers]], and Roz Dimon [[1:47:11|encourages artists to interview each other]].
