Salon 40, "Collage: Show & Tell" (March 30, 2022), is moderated by Colin Goldberg and revisits the early-salon format of short presentations. Artists each show a collage or layered piece: [[8:51|Susan Detroy]], [[12:04|Allen Hirsh, with "Turbulence" for a call for art on the ocean]], [[15:19|Ann Shapiro, whose work draws on climate maps and drawings]], [[18:15|Negin Ehtesabian, on travel restrictions for people from developing countries]], [[23:16|Michael Pierre Price, who starts from a 3D scene]], [[27:53|Patrick Lichty, with an arc of collage work]], [[39:40|Roz Dimon]], [[44:17|Malavika Mandal Andrew]], [[47:23|Renata Janiszewska]], [[50:28|Tommy Mintz]], [[54:05|Goldberg, with audio from samples and footage]] and [[1:02:26|Lee Schnaiberg]]. Afterward the group talks about [[1:12:19|the next salon's topic]], [[1:15:05|reaching people outside the group]], [[1:20:28|English subtitles for non-native speakers]] and [[1:32:06|Python as a first programming language for image work]].
