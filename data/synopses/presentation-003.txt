Presentation 3, "Collaborations Between Artists and Scientists," is moderated by Lee Day with Karen LaFleur, Nina Sobell and Cynthia Beth Rubin, and is presented with the American Maker Space in Qarshi, Uzbekistan. Each artist is introduced in turn: [[5:20|LaFleur, a full-time artist since 1975]], [[11:46|Sobell, an interactive artist since 1967]] and [[18:58|Rubin, who has worked as a computer artist since 1983]]. In the discussion that follows, Day asks [[27:00|how artists connect with scientists]] and [[30:09|how they get access to scientific equipment and institutions]]. The artists disagree about whether an artist should be [[33:10|guided by the science or free to think what they like]], talk about [[36:12|demystifying each other's data and art]] and ask [[39:29|how scientists see the artists' work]]. They also describe how scientists [[42:33|use the artwork in their own presentations]], and how [[45:34|younger scientists and abstract ideas from physics]] connect with the art.
