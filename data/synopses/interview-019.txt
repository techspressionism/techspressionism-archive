In Interview 19, Roz Dimon interviews Indian artist Malavika Mandal Andrew. Andrew describes [[6:45|growing up in Haridwar]], [[11:48|the studio space her art college provided]] and [[15:16|her first show at Jahangir Art Gallery]], and credits [[18:37|a mentor who taught her how design can be applied]]. She shows large prints, work that moved [[30:08|from photography to black and white]] and [[32:38|mixed materials that echo how her life mixed with different concepts]], and her [[35:15|digital expressionism work]]. She talks about [[43:31|galleries that questioned her subject and medium]], [[48:36|the role of white space in her pieces]] and [[53:59|making work during the lockdown]], and Dimon and Andrew reflect on [[25:00|the visual language shared with Techspressionism]] and [[58:37|the community formed through the salons]].
