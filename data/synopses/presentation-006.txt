Presentation 6, "Digital Photography," is moderated by Roz Dimon and presented with the American Makerspace in Karshi, Uzbekistan, with artists Lee Day, Gregory Little and Lee Musgrave. Dimon [[0:02|opens the session]] and shows [[9:40|her own working screen in Photoshop]]. Day, a photographer, asks [[11:06|what algorithms see]] and shows [[11:55|panoramas made by his iPhone's software]], describing his role as choosing among thousands of results. Little presents [[17:48|alternative uses of photography as reality capture]]. Musgrave shows [[24:57|how he turns photographs into Techspressionism paintings]] from collected ephemera. In the discussion, Day explains [[32:34|how the iPhone panorama function builds its images]] and [[33:35|his curatorial role]]. The group talks about [[34:03|having too many options with digital media]], [[37:40|technologies in our bloodstream]], [[39:48|the eye of the artist versus the eye of the camera]], and [[42:38|why photography is essentially reductive]], and Day ends by describing [[45:43|how places such as Tokyo and Karshi differ]].
