In Interview 21, Colin Goldberg talks with artist Victor Acevedo. Acevedo recalls [[3:29|moving to Greenpoint around 2000]] and [[6:48|working at Lamont Editions, a fine art printing facility, where he met Steve Miller]]. He describes [[11:16|his collaborative pieces]], [[22:09|taking a computer workshop in 1983]] and [[26:51|how expensive professional computer tools once were]], and agrees that [[30:35|ultimately it is the ideas, concept and feeling that count]]. He then screen-shares a slideshow of his work, including [[44:31|space-filling tilings with squares and hexagons]] and [[1:01:48|biographical images such as a photograph of his brother]]. Goldberg talks about [[1:16:01|minting his first NFTs from old digital drawings]], and Acevedo explains [[1:24:45|how he found the group through Roz Dimon]] and [[1:34:39|where to find his website]].
