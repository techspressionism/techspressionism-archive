In Interview 13, artist and teacher Cee Moses is interviewed by fellow Techspressionist Davonte Bradley. Moses talks about [[11:45|early screen grabs cut up into collage]], [[14:03|telling students that talent is not enough without work]] and the sadness and humor behind [[19:12|the heavy themes he explores]] and [[20:43|his fondness for clowns]]. He shows [[22:56|how his drawing has progressed since childhood]] and [[32:07|deeply personal photo projects about family relationships and insecurities]], and describes [[37:05|working a nude figure line by line]]. The two discuss [[42:29|the teachers who shaped them]], [[58:46|how technology lets them talk across distance]] and [[1:01:41|Moses's ties to Augusta]], then return to the art at [[1:02:57|a screen-share of his work]].
