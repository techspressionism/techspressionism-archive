In Interview 22, Roz Dimon interviews Cape Cod artist Karen LaFleur. LaFleur says art was [[5:15|a lifelong pull, long before she was six]], with stories behind everything she made, and explains [[10:05|how words were hard to get out]] during her master's work. She shows [[11:55|high school pieces]] and her ongoing [[13:52|puppetry and work on 3D gesture]], including [[21:29|puppets with fine copper-wire hair]], and talks about [[16:57|living on the islands of Cape Cod]], [[23:40|a teacher who had studied with Hans Hofmann]] and [[28:15|a computer graphics certificate at RISD]]. She discusses [[39:34|pre-pandemic and pandemic pieces such as What the Sun Said to the Moon]], [[42:05|drawing on vellum and pencil before Photoshop]] and [[46:31|how her work has been a lifetime of transition]].
