Salon 111, "Talking About Co-Working" (October 1, 2026), is moderated by Cynthia Beth Rubin and Cynthia DiDonato and focuses on Techspressionism Co-Working, the weekly, peer-run Zoom sessions the two began in July 2023. Lee Schnaiberg [[0:43|announces Salon 112, on Thursday, November 5, and its theme of perception]], and the two Cynthias [[5:41|describe how Co-Working began]] and [[6:31|how it has met every week since]]. Participating artists then describe what the group has meant to their work: Susan Detroy [[10:31|lists the perspectives and feedback she gets]], Michael Pierre Price [[14:53|compares the structure of co-working to a creative Möbius loop]], Karen LaFleur, in a pre-recorded video, [[18:49|uses it to collect her thoughts, make work and try techniques]], Lee Musgrave [[22:15|finds it humanistic rather than professorial]], and Mary Ahern, new to the group, [[25:07|says it changed how she thinks about her art]]. In pre-recorded videos, Bob Prata [[31:44|shows how Allen Hirsh's software transformed one of his drawings]] and Dimitrije Martinovic [[35:11|describes how the group eased his sense of isolation]]. Michael Woodruff [[38:07|shows what co-working achieves cumulatively]], Allen Hirsh [[41:10|uses fellow artists' work as a platform for his own transformations]], Stephen Paré [[45:47|shows a picture that proved decisive for him]], and cha [[48:27|talks about sharing work from a prolific practice]], including [[51:23|a series of about 99 videos]]. Cynthia DiDonato [[54:41|reads a statement from Sahar Moussavi]], who could not attend, and Marya Triandafellos [[57:11|speaks in a pre-recorded video about the benefit to her practice]]. Cynthia Beth Rubin [[59:56|describes collaborations that grew out of co-working]], and Cynthia DiDonato [[1:05:25|presents on artist influences and "breaking the border"]] before the Cynthias [[1:08:31|explain how to join]]. In the open discussion, Stephen Carpenter [[1:14:58|says he hopes to join once his schedule allows]], Allen Hirsh [[1:17:29|says no other group lets him discuss technology in art]], and Susan Detroy [[1:20:48|says co-working and the salons changed who she is as an artist]]. Colin Goldberg [[1:23:29|thanks the Cynthias and all the participants]] and [[1:32:05|invites everyone to the after-party, where ideas for upcoming salons are discussed]].
