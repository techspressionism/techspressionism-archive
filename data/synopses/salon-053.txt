Salon 53, "Photography and Media Confluence" (September 28, 2022), is co-moderated by Tommy Mintz and Renata Janiszewska and asks whether photography will die as painting was once said to. Tikoi Kuitenbrouwer [[4:06|says no]], and Clive Holden [[11:54|reflects on the history of seeing dramatic changes in the art world]]. Verneda Lights presents [[17:03|the innovation of color]] and [[26:03|a self-portrait made after her mother's death]]. Allen Hirsh [[38:22|shows iPhone images manipulated in Procreate]], Cynthia Beth Rubin [[45:10|shows morphing work and a window installation using space-telescope images]], and Colin Goldberg [[52:31|shows a series that combines photographic elements with drawing and painting]]. Mintz [[1:07:55|presents on a very large display in a gallery]]. The discussion covers [[1:20:06|new wall-size displays]], [[1:25:57|photography and access for people who could not afford it]], [[1:32:44|earlier debates about whether photography is still photography]] and [[1:36:21|how displays handle color]].
