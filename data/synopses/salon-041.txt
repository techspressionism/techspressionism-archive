Salon 41, "Climate: Show & Tell" (April 13, 2022), is moderated by Davonte Bradley and is the last salon before the first large physical exhibition in Southampton. Karen LaFleur [[3:53|shows work toward a solo show themed on the ocean]], and Ann Shapiro [[12:31|shows her first computer drawing, from 1989, as her muse]], then talks about [[22:36|smaller works that travelled to a museum]]. Susan Detroy [[49:56|shares work about nature and flowers]], and Lee Schnaiberg [[59:24|describes making work about climate change without overwhelming viewers]] and [[1:02:53|how climate change is unfolding faster than predicted]]. Detroy [[1:08:25|adds a note on watching the salon on her television]], and the group closes with [[1:20:47|comments on the artists' work]].
