Salon 80 (March 7, 2024), moderated by Renata Janiszewska, is on the topic of art and money. Susan Detroy [[6:15|describes her work and her teaching of transfer techniques]], and Colin Goldberg [[14:14|shares what he found while taking inventory of his work]]. Cari Ann Shim Sham [[24:26|and Joey Zaza introduce a museum project]], with Zaza [[31:57|describing how artists price and sell work]]. Jan Swinburne [[39:32|presents on money and NFTs]], and Roz Dimon [[50:42|asks "what is money but value?"]]. In the discussion, Lee Musgrave [[1:01:21|draws on eight decades of watching the art market rise and fall]], Verneda Lights argues that [[1:06:59|the art industry is built on exploitation]], Stephen Carpenter [[1:20:29|comments as a first-time attendee]], and Cynthia Beth Rubin [[1:24:42|discusses international and cultural hierarchies]].
