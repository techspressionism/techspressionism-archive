Salon 8 (January 5, 2021), the first recorded salon, moderated by Davonte Bradley, features six artists. Frédéric Pons, a motion designer in France, [[3:30|describes turning to art and Instagram glitch apps]]. Giovanna Sun [[11:00|presents ArtPot, a mobile app for AR and VR galleries]]. Michael Pointer shows [[23:43|"In Signal Noise," photographs of broken television signals]]. Malavika Mandal Andrew [[31:52|describes her work after textile design training in India]]. Lucy Boyd-Wilson [[38:55|introduces her interactive and VR work]], and Karen LaFleur [[47:55|presents her "sudden fiction" micro-stories combined with art and music]]. The rest of the salon is open discussion about [[1:06:09|building a community]], [[1:11:53|a virtual exhibition followed by a physical show]], [[1:17:00|including artists from beyond the US, UK and Canada]], [[1:44:25|meeting weekly to work together]] and [[1:55:53|using Instagram Reels]].
