Salon 67 (April 12, 2023) is an open discussion, mostly about AI-generated imagery. Verneda Lights [[22:06|argues that demanding proof of "the hand" is another barrier to entry]] and [[34:44|discusses image-generation tools such as NightCafe]]. Colin Goldberg [[53:30|describes using AI for illustration, including invitations for the salons]], and Susan Detroy [[58:20|says she has put her AI project aside]]. The group also [[1:10:21|discusses Egon Schiele]] and Lee Schnaiberg [[1:28:20|recalls curating work before the move to digital]].
