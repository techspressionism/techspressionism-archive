Salon 20 (June 22, 2021), moderated by Davonte Bradley, features Victor Acevedo and Christine Sciulli. Acevedo [[6:12|presents his career in digital art]], including [[15:15|early 3D modeling with a collaborator]], [[24:26|an image used as the cover of Leonardo in 2001]], [[33:59|a piece playing on Richard Hamilton's 1956 collage]] and [[54:52|the snapshot-like looseness of his recent work]]. Sciulli [[58:55|then presents]], describing [[1:08:37|a project with Frederica Taylor]] and [[1:18:29|an installation at Guild Hall in East Hampton]]. The closing discussion looks back at [[1:45:18|when it was called computer art rather than digital art]].
