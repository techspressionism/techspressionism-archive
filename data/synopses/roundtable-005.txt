Roundtable 5, "Four Techspressionist Artists," is moderated by Michael Pierre Price with Colin Goldberg, Renata Janiszewska, Karen LaFleur and Jan Swinburne, whose work is in an exhibition at 150 Media Stream in Chicago. The session opens with [[0:07|an AI voice (ChatGPT) asked about the exhibition, its curator and the artists]]. The artists then talk about [[8:05|what brought them to Techspressionism]], starting with [[11:00|LaFleur's early access to computers]], [[20:02|Swinburne's childhood among religious iconography in Montreal]] and [[23:08|Goldberg's lifelong love of drawing]], and about the teachers and early influences who [[26:29|encouraged them]] and [[31:18|shaped their paths]]. The discussion turns to how their practices develop over time: [[43:03|Swinburne compares her process to an avalanche]] and [[46:58|LaFleur compares hers to octopus arms]]. It closes with [[52:07|what each hopes to do next]], including [[55:28|Goldberg's plans for his own work and for the Techspressionism community]].
