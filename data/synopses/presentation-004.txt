Presentation 4, "Digital Painting & Drawing 2," is the second session in a series presented with the American Maker Space in Qarshi, Uzbekistan, and is hosted by Gregory Little. Little opens with [[0:00|a definition of digital drawing and painting]], [[3:20|the Hello Uzbekistan exhibition that led to the series]] and [[5:50|early computer-graphics pioneers]]. Colin Goldberg then presents [[8:53|his path from traditional drawing and painting to Techspressionism]]. A second artist shows [[16:35|how a video shown in Urgench was built with iPad apps and free tools]], and Cynthia Beth Rubin describes [[22:03|how her painting background shapes her digital work]]. In the discussion, the artists compare [[30:16|digital and traditional processes]], [[33:21|printers and a laser cutter at the makerspace]], [[36:21|working across many iPad apps]] and [[39:27|mixing physical and digital media]]. They explain [[42:31|vector versus raster graphics]] and [[45:41|free tools such as Inkscape and GIMP]], talk about [[48:44|choosing where to put your energy]] and [[55:01|meeting across time zones]], and discuss [[58:08|building worlds in Minecraft]]. They end with [[1:04:16|encouragement to beginners to join the co-working sessions]].
