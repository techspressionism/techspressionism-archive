Salon 68, "Appropriation" (April 26, 2023), is moderated by Colin Goldberg. Rebecca Tombaugh [[10:09|joins from Kansas City, Missouri]], and Cynthia Beth Rubin [[28:14|discusses a paper she wrote in 1992]] on cultural appropriation. Lee Schnaiberg [[47:14|responds to Rubin's presentation]], and the group discusses [[59:31|media, advertising and appropriation]], [[1:11:45|the difficulty of selling NFTs]] and [[1:28:00|the Middle Ages and the history of algebra]].
