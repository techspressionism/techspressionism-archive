Salon 74, "Taking Stock: Open Mic" (September 7, 2023), is moderated by Renata Janiszewska and asks about the future direction of Techspressionism. Anton Dubrovin, a digital artist born in Kazakhstan, [[6:53|introduces himself and his work]]. Cynthia Beth Rubin [[23:52|shows her work]], and the group discusses [[48:26|rough dates for a possible project]] and [[1:01:07|an idea for the group to try]]. Sahar Moussavi [[1:09:53|presents images from recent co-working sessions]], Susan Detroy [[1:15:09|shows the whole process of making a piece]], and Lee Schnaiberg [[1:22:21|shows the software he uses to turn his paintings into videos]].
