Salon 35 (January 18, 2022) features three presenters. Lily Kostrzewa, who came from Taiwan, [[1:24|shares her art journey]], including [[21:48|cross-cultural work in Chinese ink]], [[33:17|losing her parents in 2017 and 2018]] and [[43:40|merging two styles]]. The next presenter describes [[54:13|reworking photographs sent by other people]] and [[1:14:46|writing pastoral poetry in response]], and the discussion asks [[1:24:58|whether the poetry or the art comes first]]. Michael Pierre Price presents [[1:35:25|fractal-looking images]] and talks about [[1:45:41|the precision of mathematics compared with the imprecision of language]].
