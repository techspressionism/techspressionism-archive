In Interview 12, Patrick Lichty interviews Gregory Little, a mixed-media, digital artist, painter and sculptor in Oberlin, Ohio. Little recalls [[3:45|his undergraduate years trying to outpaint de Kooning]] and [[5:45|becoming the person who could draw the map]]. He shows [[18:35|early virtual reality worlds coded to fit on a handheld device]], [[24:37|his current work in new environments]] and [[31:00|a painting layered over a digital print]], plus [[33:27|assets made in a 3D iPad app that no longer exists]] and [[35:25|a plan for a layered virtual reality piece]]. The two discuss [[26:22|whether the machine is something other than human]], [[28:54|the pioneers who popularized creative VR]], [[40:45|presence in large-scale VR]] and [[44:18|why surrealism was absorbed so easily into design and advertising]]. They also touch on [[45:25|short digital loops]] and Walter Benjamin's ideas about [[46:41|the work of art]].
