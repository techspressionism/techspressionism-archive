In Interview 26, artist Renata Janiszewska interviews digital art pioneer Nina Sobell, who shows a presentation she gave for the Brooklyn Rail. Sobell recalls [[2:12|her upbringing on Long Island and in New York]] and [[16:46|seeing her first Giacomettis at MoMA as a child]], then presents her work on [[4:58|visualizing non-verbal communication through the brain's electrochemistry]]. She describes [[10:38|the 1992 brainwave drawing installation at the Bronx River Arts Gallery]], [[12:29|EEG seances linking artists in Banff and New York]] and [[21:07|the alpha machine that inspired the brainwave drawings]]. She also covers [[43:44|Aleph, performances made from 2016 to 2018]] and [[50:53|her use of phone apps beginning in 2015]].
