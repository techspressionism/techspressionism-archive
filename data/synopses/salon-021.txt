Salon 21 (July 6, 2021), moderated by Davonte Bradley, features Cynthia Beth Rubin and Renata Janiszewska. Rubin [[4:18|opens with recent pieces]] and discusses [[15:26|ideas drawn from a manuscript where many things happen at once]], [[25:09|an artist residency and the idea of putting herself in the water]] and [[57:43|a film that was shown at major film festivals]]. Janiszewska [[1:04:58|presents her recent animation work, influenced by Norman McLaren, Colin Campbell and Jan Swinburne]], and [[1:14:22|explains her interest in Bauhaus typography]]. The discussion covers [[1:23:40|her painting-program techniques]] and [[1:33:26|the difficulty of dealing with gallerists in 2011]].
