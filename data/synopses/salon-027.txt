Salon 27 (September 28, 2021) features Holly Gordon and moderator Davonte Bradley, who presents his own work. Gordon [[2:55|describes how an article about the painter Albert Pinkham Ryder led her into art]], talks about [[21:32|using camera filters to "recreate the visible"]] and [[30:59|her connection with Arthur Dove, considered by some historians the first American abstract expressionist]], and shows [[1:00:12|work that draws on her youth making art on black backgrounds]]. Bradley shows [[1:10:07|a piece with a web of lines and shapes]] and [[1:28:38|a palette of gold and earth tones with blues referring to the Afrofuturist movement]]. The salon ends with [[1:49:28|questions about the modalities he might expand on]].
