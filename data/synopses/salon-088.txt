Salon 88, "Education" (November 7, 2024), is organized by Malavika Mandal Andrew, who brings students from India into the salon. Andrew [[3:16|welcomes the audience and introduces the students' work]], and the students present, including Anya Chaturvedi [[19:42|who is new to digital art]], Adrita Purkait [[29:49|with a collaborative drawing of dreams]] and Anaya Shah [[37:52|who added a sky to a partner's piece]]. Colin Goldberg [[44:23|comments on the students' ability to explain their work in English]]. Sahar Moussavi [[52:17|presents a workshop on applying AI to jewelry design]], Roz Dimon [[1:04:23|discusses her art school in Athens, Georgia]], and Cynthia Beth Rubin [[1:09:18|shows an assignment she developed about thirty years ago]] and [[1:12:11|describes teaching in a regular computer lab]].
