Salon 98 (September 4, 2025), hosted by Colin Goldberg, is on the topic of "phygital," work that combines physical and digital. Ann Shapiro [[9:02|shows climate-change work]], Roz Dimon [[21:01|talks about the word and her own approach]], and Goldberg [[34:07|shows painting combined with digital printmaking]], including [[47:26|a six-by-eight-foot digital monoprint on vinyl]]. Lucy Boyd-Wilson [[54:34|describes work with a Kinect motion-tracking camera]], Susan Detroy [[58:56|describes an analog project and a digital one]], and Malavika Mandal Andrew [[1:08:34|discusses her digital work]]. The salon ends with [[1:22:01|a pointer to the Every Woman Biennial site]].
