Salon 107, "New Artists" (June 4, 2026), is a new format hosted by Colin Goldberg in which four artists recently added to the Techspressionist Visual Artist Index (more than 350 artists from over 45 countries) present their work. Tina Anderson, from Moscow, [[1:00|describes building "sacred machinery" and her series of impossible objects]]. Another artist [[13:28|talks about the brain as a pattern-recognition machine]] and [[26:32|goes back to work begun in 2019]]. Beau Tardy, from Houston, [[40:02|is introduced as the last presenter]] and later [[53:24|recalls his years as an artist on Varick Street]]. The discussion covers [[1:06:47|a love-hate relationship with technology]] and [[1:20:00|working as an inventor]], and Goldberg closes with [[1:33:27|what he thinks the group has to offer]].
