Salon 93, "Live from Cape Cod" (April 3, 2025), is a hybrid salon combining the online audience with a live audience at the Cape Cod Museum of Art. A presentation traces [[13:41|the development of Techspressionism.com]], [[27:32|an exhibition curated for the Southampton Arts Center]] and [[40:56|Duchamp's idea that the viewer completes the work]]. Artists then discuss their work, including [[54:19|an early iteration of a piece]] and [[1:07:24|a piece made for Cape Cod residents]]. The salon ends with [[1:20:49|encouragement to use Techspressionism as a way to organize projects]].
