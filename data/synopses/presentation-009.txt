Presentation 9, "Technology and Art with Colin Goldberg," is a virtual program from the Pollock-Krasner House and Study Center in East Hampton, recorded in February 2021 and led by Joyce Raimondo, with an introduction by Helen A. Harrison. Harrison opens with [[0:09|artists who went against the grain, from the Post-Impressionists on]]. Raimondo then discusses [[4:43|the home and studio of Jackson Pollock and Lee Krasner]] and shows [[15:34|a film of Pollock painting]], and asks the audience what they see in [[18:43|a Pollock painting]], concluding that the viewer completes it. Goldberg presents [[29:21|his work and the origins of Techspressionism]], [[41:47|a piece that refers to a poem his father used to recite]] and [[46:09|a tour of his studio]], including [[51:18|his plotter]] and [[53:41|the gesso he applies before printing photographs onto canvas]]. In the Q&A, he talks about [[55:51|his printers]], [[59:17|what response he hopes for from viewers]], [[1:03:51|what "wireframe" means]] and [[1:05:38|crypto art and NFTs]]. Raimondo raises [[1:08:21|the question of whether new work is called "not art"]]. Audience members Diane Marsella, Roz Dimon, Darcy Gerbarg and Tommy Mintz [[1:11:34|share their own work]], and the program ends with [[1:28:26|how to follow Goldberg's work]].
