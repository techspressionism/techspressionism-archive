Salon 89 (December 5, 2024) is presented by Renata Janiszewska and Karen LaFleur. Janiszewska [[7:03|presents from Lion's Head, Canada]] and describes [[20:35|her emotional response to stories rather than the stories themselves]]. LaFleur [[28:16|gives two talks in one]], including [[42:31|work on whirlpools and how they are studied]], and [[56:02|advises artists on showing work in their own region]]. The discussion covers [[1:09:26|commercial animation studios such as Pixar]] and [[1:22:27|the venues that will and won't show an artist's work]].
