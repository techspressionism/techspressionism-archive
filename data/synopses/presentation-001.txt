Presentation 1, "Digital Painting and Drawing 1," is hosted by Cynthia Beth Rubin and presented virtually with the American Maker Space in Qarshi, Uzbekistan. It features Roz Dimon, Ann Shapiro and Stephen Paré. Rubin introduces the session and Roz Dimon [[8:51|who has made digital art since the 1980s]], then Ann Shapiro [[14:20|who came to painting while working in higher education]], and Stephen Paré [[22:04|who began in music and acting]]. After [[29:05|thanks to the hosts]], the artists take part in a discussion. Topics include [[33:40|how digital tools have changed since they began]], [[43:06|sharing work across the world in a language everyone can join]] and how [[46:14|childhood and place]] show up in their art. One artist describes [[55:32|how the wall of the computer screen feels at first, and then stops feeling like one]]. Rubin closes by [[58:34|inviting the audience to register at the Techspressionism site]].
