Salon 45, "...and Beyond: Show & Tell" (June 8, 2022), is hosted by Colin Goldberg. Renata Janiszewska [[5:22|describes the new Canadian node of Techspressionism]], and artists then show recent work: Giovanna Sun [[14:48|on a program that generates images from typed sentences]], [[19:48|Tommy Mintz]], Michael Rees [[25:21|on a conference he organized]], [[28:46|Holly Gordon]], Lucy Boyd-Wilson [[40:18|on a VR and full-dome piece]] and [[52:31|Ellis Eclipse, a virtual photographer, in a first show and tell]]. Goldberg closes by noting [[1:20:59|that Instagram chats are a good way to gather people, which is how the nodes began]].
