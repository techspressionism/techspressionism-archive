Salon 76, "Phreaking" (November 2, 2023), is moderated by Lee Schnaiberg and looks at hacking, phone systems and the early history of computing. The opening discussion covers [[15:41|how early computer makers borrowed the graphical interface from Xerox]]. Artists then show related work: Renata Janiszewska [[26:16|on shapes made with iOS 17]], Colin Goldberg [[30:58|on a Polycam 3D capture]], [[39:07|Allen Hirsh]], [[45:16|Schnaiberg, on a payphone near his house]], Lee Day [[48:03|on how a phone's software can go wrong]], [[1:01:18|feedback loops in audio and visuals]] and Tommy Mintz [[1:05:16|on a browser-based 3D space built on Mozilla Hubs]]. The session closes with [[1:33:50|the choice of a topic for the next salon]].
