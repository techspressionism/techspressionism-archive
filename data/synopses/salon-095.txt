Salon 95, "Live from Chelsea" (June 5, 2025), is a hybrid salon from the Hudson Guild Gallery in the Hello Chelsea exhibition, hosted by Tommy Mintz. The discussion is about community. Roz Dimon [[3:55|describes a group she started called Picture Element]] and [[10:40|spending half her time online]]. Carla Gannis [[16:45|talks about scarcity and the Madagascar Institute]], and Cynthia Beth Rubin [[21:42|describes a planned trip to the Museum of Contemporary Art in Uzbekistan]]. Colin Goldberg [[26:38|compares Techspressionism with past movements bound by style]] and [[55:20|says NFTs upend the art world's economic structure]]. Dimon [[37:42|discusses the move from biweekly to monthly meetings]], and Seungjin Lee [[42:25|talks about her work at the Met]]. Gannis [[1:06:04|discusses AI in the classroom]].
