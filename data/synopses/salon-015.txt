Salon 15 (April 13, 2021) features three artists, followed by a presentation of a collaborative exhibition. Andrea Bonaceto [[1:48|presents his work]], including [[29:22|the idea of second-life worlds]]. Tommy Mintz [[32:42|presents a slide show]] about [[42:12|photographing with a computer and light stand]], and closes with [[51:39|thoughts on photography]]. Clive Holden [[1:07:05|describes forty years of making work]], beginning with experimental films, and answers [[1:22:34|a question about Anni Albers]] and [[1:31:54|the possibilities of NFTs]]. The salon then moves into [[1:36:05|the first Techspressionist collaboration exhibition]], where artists talk about [[1:45:22|working in pairs]] and [[2:03:50|rebuilding a fractal from tens of thousands of primitive shapes]].
