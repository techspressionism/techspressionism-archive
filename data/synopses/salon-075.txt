Salon 75, "Falling Leaves" (October 5, 2023), marks three years of salons and is moderated by Michael Pierre Price. Allen Hirsh [[6:58|talks about Procreate]] and shows [[19:25|images from television glitches]]. The group then discusses [[43:48|opening the group to students]] and [[55:54|the importance of having many participants]]. Susan Detroy [[1:06:55|reports on a prize-winning biking film and a MOCA Lights exhibition]], and Lee Schnaiberg [[1:17:06|shows footage shot that morning with leaves in mind]].
