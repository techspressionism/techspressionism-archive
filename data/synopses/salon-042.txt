Salon 42, "Techspressionism: Digital and Beyond, International Reception" (April 27, 2022), is a long reception for the Southampton exhibition, moderated by Davonte Bradley. Artists in the show talk about their pieces: Patrick Lichty [[10:15|on the cover image of the 2021 global exhibition]], Renata Janiszewska [[16:53|on narrative in her titles]], Mary Ann Strandell [[26:48|on 3D lenticular prints]], Cynthia Beth Rubin [[33:36|on combining plankton and Hebrew manuscripts]], [[1:04:53|Victor Acevedo]], Karen LaFleur [[1:07:59|on an animation]], Lee Schnaiberg [[1:12:25|on panorama sabotage work]], [[1:22:32|Giovanna Sun]], Verneda Lights [[1:26:56|who had attended the exhibition in Southampton]], [[1:31:06|Lee Musgrave]], [[1:35:59|Nina Sobell]], Darcy Gerbarg [[1:42:23|on her Inside and Outside series]], Negin Ehtesabian [[1:45:20|on pieces about Iran and America]], [[2:07:34|ScoJo]] and Roz Dimon [[2:12:25|on her "Kalashnikov II" in solidarity with Ukraine]]. Colin Goldberg also reads statements from artists who could not speak, such as [[45:00|a generative artist who throws paint particles at random]] and [[1:48:25|a series called the Toxic Garden]], and [[2:15:35|announces the events planned through the exhibition's close on June 4]].
