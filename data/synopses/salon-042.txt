Salon 42, "Techspressionism: Digital and Beyond, International Reception" (April 27, 2022), is a long reception for the Southampton exhibition, moderated by Davonte Bradley. Artists in the show talk about their pieces: [[10:15|Patrick Lichty, on the cover image of the 2021 global exhibition]], [[16:53|Renata Janiszewska, on narrative in her titles]], [[26:48|Mary Ann Strandell, on 3D lenticular prints]], [[33:36|Cynthia Beth Rubin, on combining plankton and Hebrew manuscripts]], [[1:04:53|Victor Acevedo]], [[1:07:59|Karen LaFleur, on an animation]], [[1:12:25|Lee Schnaiberg, on panorama sabotage work]], [[1:22:32|Giovanna Sun]], [[1:26:56|Verneda Lights, who had attended the exhibition in Southampton]], [[1:31:06|Lee Musgrave]], [[1:35:59|Nina Sobell]], [[1:42:23|Darcy Gerbarg, on her Inside and Outside series]], [[1:45:20|Negin Ehtesabian, on pieces about Iran and America]], [[2:07:34|ScoJo]] and [[2:12:25|Roz Dimon, on her "Kalashnikov II" in solidarity with Ukraine]]. Colin Goldberg also reads statements from artists who could not speak, such as [[45:00|a generative artist who throws paint particles at random]] and [[1:48:25|a series called the Toxic Garden]], and [[2:15:35|announces the events planned through the exhibition's close on June 4]].
