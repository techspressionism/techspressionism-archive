Salon 43, "Femme Tech" (May 11, 2022), is moderated by Roz Dimon as part of the Techspressionism "Digital and Beyond" exhibition. Each artist speaks for about ten minutes on their work, women in technology and Techspressionism. Speakers include [[4:50|Anne Morgan Spalter, who describes her work and a Sotheby's auction she co-curated]], [[14:01|Nina Sobell]], [[22:16|Cynthia Beth Rubin, on the pieces in the show]], [[34:36|Verneda Lights]], [[47:19|Karen LaFleur, on communication]], [[54:32|Renata Janiszewska]], [[1:01:26|Darcy Gerbarg]], [[1:06:45|Negin Ehtesabian, on art being honest to reality]], [[1:17:37|Giovanna Sun]] and [[1:25:36|Allen Hirsh, on women's early involvement in technology]]. Dimon [[1:31:35|thanks the panel]], and Colin Goldberg notes [[1:37:23|new Techspressionist nodes forming in other countries]].
