Salon 65, "Revolution" (March 15, 2023), is moderated by Davonte Bradley. Tommy Mintz [[3:20|opens the presentations]], followed by Colin Goldberg, who [[33:52|shows work he dug out from the archives]], and Cynthia DiDonato, who [[45:07|shows several pieces]]. Bradley [[56:56|shares his thoughts on revolution from the perspective of a Black artist]] and shows [[1:09:08|a piece called "Lip Service" about political parties]]. The closing discussion asks [[1:24:38|whether it is fair to ask an artist to justify their art]].
