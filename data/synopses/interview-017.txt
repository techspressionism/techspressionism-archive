In Interview 17, Michael Pierre Price interviews photographer and collage artist Tommy Mintz. Mintz recommends [[4:31|Robin Kelsey's book Photography and the Art of Chance]] and discusses [[7:12|the formal side of his recent collages]], [[14:59|street photography among people with cameras]] and [[25:58|the influence of being a New Yorker]]. He explains [[22:38|immersive prints that surround the viewer]], [[23:07|measuring change in a location between photographs]] and [[31:17|making very large prints]], and shows [[36:06|zooming through a face]] and [[41:28|a collage video of a dog's day]]. He talks about [[38:39|photography's changing aesthetics]], [[50:29|the tension between a cold process and human choices]] and [[55:26|what Techspressionism offers the artist community]].
