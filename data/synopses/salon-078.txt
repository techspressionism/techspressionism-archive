Salon 78 (January 4, 2024), moderated by Michael Pierre Price, is on the topic of text and art. Karen LaFleur [[3:51|opens with a wide-ranging presentation]], including [[7:23|a passage on drawing versus reading]]. Seth Indigo Carnes [[28:49|presents his work]], including [[34:18|how authorship changes over time in collaborative and interactive pieces]]. Sherry Karver [[47:09|shows crowd scenes with text attached to individuals]] and [[51:02|explains the gray areas]]. Renata Janiszewska [[58:12|comments on the set]], and Carnes discusses [[1:10:39|his public app]] and [[1:16:45|whether it could support other languages]]. Colin Goldberg [[1:24:29|closes with a note on connections through mutual colleagues]].
