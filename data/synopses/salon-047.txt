Salon 47, "Materiality" (July 6, 2022), is moderated by Michael Pierre Price. Artists show work and talk about physical materials in digital practice: [[4:49|Jan Swinburne, on carved and printed versions of the word "love"]], [[10:12|Renata Janiszewska, on organic form with no space behind it]], [[14:50|Colin Goldberg, on papers and India ink grounds for his plotter drawings]], [[19:26|Patrick Lichty]], [[38:31|Susan Detroy, on gathering a collection prompted by NFTs]] and [[1:04:32|Malavika Mandal Andrew, on acrylic sheet engraved with threads]]. The discussion covers [[59:38|how process drives experiment with the plotter]], [[1:11:57|essays on avant-garde and kitsch]], [[1:16:33|how the pandemic pushed artists away from screens]] and [[1:21:25|curators who understand digital work]].
