Salon 47, "Materiality" (July 6, 2022), is moderated by Michael Pierre Price. Artists show work and talk about physical materials in digital practice: Jan Swinburne [[4:49|on carved and printed versions of the word "love"]], Renata Janiszewska [[10:12|on organic form with no space behind it]], Colin Goldberg [[14:50|on papers and India ink grounds for his plotter drawings]], [[19:26|Patrick Lichty]], Susan Detroy [[38:31|on gathering a collection prompted by NFTs]] and Malavika Mandal Andrew [[1:04:32|on acrylic sheet engraved with threads]]. The discussion covers [[59:38|how process drives experiment with the plotter]], [[1:11:57|essays on avant-garde and kitsch]], [[1:16:33|how the pandemic pushed artists away from screens]] and [[1:21:25|curators who understand digital work]].
