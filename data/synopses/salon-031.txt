Salon 31 (November 23, 2021), moderated by Davonte Bradley, features artist Carmen (Cee) Moses and guests from the NFT community. Moses [[7:35|presents her manipulated photographs]], including [[16:39|a portrait of her daughter in a skull mask]]. Two guests then explain NFTs and Web3 for artists, including [[56:57|how they hope to help artists get to market]], [[1:06:16|why no single organization controls the blockchain]] and [[1:15:37|cross-chain compatibility]]. The salon closes with [[1:34:01|an artist's story of a collector]] and [[1:53:20|practical advice on sending files]].
