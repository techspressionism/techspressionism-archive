Salon 112, "Perception" (November 5, 2026), is moderated by Lee Schnaiberg, who [[0:25|introduces the theme]] and describes how [[1:09|the brain builds the reality we see from a flood of sensory data]]. In a pre-recorded video, Karen LaFleur [[3:11|reflects on perception through the memoir she is writing]], [[7:33|compares the uncanny aliveness of an artwork to a puppet that holds our attention while we ignore its strings]], [[10:21|contrasts puppets with dolls]] and [[13:56|describes perception between the viewer and the viewed as a two-way loop]]. Michael Pierre Price [[18:10|brings together physics, neuroscience, consciousness and spirituality]], explaining that [[21:01|the act of seeing is an act of creation]]; he [[28:32|thinks of consciousness as a Möbius loop]], [[32:11|shows Two Up Quarks and One Down Quark in the Higgs Field]], [[33:50|describes his time with Indigenous elders and his psilocybin journeys]] and [[40:04|closes with the vast ocean of oneness]]. Lee Schnaiberg [[42:00|recalls that the Salon's original topic was psychedelica]] and [[44:48|speaks of rummaging through the drawers of perception]]. Karen LaFleur [[45:36|joins live]]. Lee Schnaiberg then shows his own work, telling how [[47:28|a drawing class that asked students to draw one unbroken line]] and [[49:05|a message he began to write while on mushrooms]] led to [[50:53|decades of graphite line drawings]]; he [[55:11|explains bottom-up and top-down messaging in the brain]], and [[59:16|shows his folded rainbows]] and videos that [[1:00:17|make it harder to tell foreground from background]]. Keana Sears, a 3D artist, [[1:02:54|draws on psychedelic experiences]], [[1:04:20|plays several animations]] and [[1:08:27|talks about the Mesoamerican, Shipibo and Indian geometry in the work]] and [[1:09:01|the perennial imagery that flows through cultures]]. Karen LaFleur [[1:17:10|says she is now 3D scanning the hands and faces from her early dolls for her moving-image work]] and [[1:18:39|tells how she was halfway to Woodstock]]. Colin Goldberg [[1:21:47|thanks Lee Schnaiberg]], [[1:22:22|plays the reel teaser for the Paris exhibition]], [[1:24:58|announces next month's Salon on India, moderated by Malavika]] and [[1:25:42|invites everyone to the after-party]].
