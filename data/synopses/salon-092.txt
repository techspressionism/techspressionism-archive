Salon 92, "Breaking AI" (March 6, 2025), is hosted by Colin Goldberg. Ariel Barron-Robbins [[6:24|presents from a college classroom]] and explains [[15:10|the rules she sets for her projects, influenced by Fluxus]]. Systaime [[21:20|performs and presents work about circuits and recycled waste]], and Michael Pierre Price shows [[38:28|how he created AI-generated backdrops for his diagrams]] and [[53:42|the challenge of printing AI images that come out small]]. Randi Matushevitz [[43:34|discusses contemporary portraiture with AI]]. Goldberg notes that [[49:24|many artists describe working with AI as a collaboration]] and ends by [[1:31:19|welcoming more non-English-speaking artists]].
