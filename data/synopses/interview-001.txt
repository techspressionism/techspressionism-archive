In Interview 1, artist Roz Dimon interviews Colin Goldberg, who coined the term Techspressionism in 2011. He describes [[0:55|growing up on the East End of Long Island]] to parents who were both chemists, [[2:23|how drawing became a lifelong path]] and how [[8:12|his grandmother's Japanese calligraphy shaped his painting]]. He explains [[13:36|what crypto art is]] and shares a work in progress that [[15:34|layers oil painting, digital manipulation and gestural marks]]. He also talks about [[21:45|his MFA in computer art at Bowling Green]], [[23:11|walking through his work by series]] from early painting to [[26:03|the digital Metagraphs of 1999 to 2005]], and [[30:57|how Techspressionism began as the title of a 2011 solo show]] and grew into a community, including its idea of [[36:16|a social sculpture]]. He closes with [[37:33|how digital tools have changed what it means to be an artist]].
