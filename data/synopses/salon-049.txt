Salon 49, "Movement" (August 3, 2022), is a discussion led by Colin Goldberg on whether and how Techspressionism functions as a movement. Michael Pierre Price notes that [[10:47|historically, movements tend to form among people in close contact]]. Goldberg says the term has become [[19:20|a vehicle for artists]], and Cynthia Beth Rubin and Patrick Lichty [[22:40|discuss what collectives offer artists]] and [[29:58|their own experience in collectives]]. Goldberg then covers Steve Miller [[34:08|'s link to Pollock and the East End]], [[42:11|the press page on the site]], [[51:54|the social accounts]] and [[1:29:40|the partnership with the Kunstmatrix platform]]. Other participants talk about [[54:55|what the group has meant to them]], [[58:39|the space art creates]], [[1:13:49|artist interviews and handshake events]] and [[1:32:49|the lack of critique outside an MFA program]].
