Salon 62, "Art Pirates" (February 1, 2023), takes up appropriation, piracy and originality. Zachary Zaitzeff [[2:14|introduces himself and describes about thirty years of making and selling art]] and [[14:17|the research behind a flag piece]]. Steve Miller [[37:15|discusses appropriation, including Richard Prince's enlargements of images from a book]]. The group also discusses [[49:44|piracy in art]] and [[1:02:36|the worry that an AI bot could suppress human work]]. Colin Goldberg [[1:05:50|recalls trading pirated video games on his Commodore 64]], and the salon ends with [[1:18:12|whether transforming a photograph into a sculpture counts as transformation]].
