Salon 23 (August 3, 2021), moderated by Davonte Bradley, features Rebecca Tombaugh and Cynthia DiDonato. Tombaugh [[4:50|presents her work]], including [[14:16|a piece made from a sketch of a flower]], and answers questions about [[34:23|resolution]]. DiDonato [[54:30|presents her digital work made with Procreate and other programs]], showing [[1:03:36|"All That Glitters Is Gold"]], [[1:12:50|"Don't Look Back," prompted by events at the border]] and [[1:22:03|"Route 66," made from a slow-shutter photograph of a car]]. She then explains [[1:31:07|how she prints her pieces]] and [[1:40:47|how she handles colors that fall outside the printer's range]].
