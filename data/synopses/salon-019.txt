Salon 19 (June 8, 2021), moderated by Davonte Bradley, features Gregory Little and Lee Musgrave. Little [[3:10|presents his work]], recalling [[13:01|two summers spent learning to build virtual reality]], [[22:30|an installation with a theremin hung upside down from the ceiling]] and [[32:34|an animation that has been exhibited and performed live in several formats]]. Musgrave [[40:29|presents his work]], describing how [[49:46|he continued photography, moving from weathered walls to nature]], and showing [[59:01|"Fiddle Diddle with Red Bean," an image from found junk that has appeared in many exhibits]]. The discussion covers [[1:08:18|working by waiting to be inspired]], [[1:37:10|upcoming exhibitions]] and [[1:51:54|breaking through the wall between artists and audiences]].
