Salon 100, "Hello Uzbekistan" (September 2025), is a milestone salon about the group's exhibition and workshops in Uzbekistan, moderated in part by Annette Weintraub. Participating artists each present their piece and their experience, in alphabetical order: [[18:03|Cynthia Beth Rubin, who works with plankton]], [[22:45|Gregory Little, from Oberlin, Ohio]], [[27:13|Gregory Patrick Garvey, who describes how the project began in 2023]], [[31:18|Lee Day, with "Clipping Suburbia, Japan"]], [[36:05|Michael Woodruff, from London, in his first exhibition printing the work]], [[41:28|Negin Ehtesabian and Patrick Lichty, with "The House on the Water"]], [[46:15|Roz Dimon, on how she came to Uzbekistan through Rubin]] and [[51:26|Stephen Paré, on spreading the word through social media]]. The salon ends with [[1:36:19|an invitation to viewers of the recording to join the group]].
