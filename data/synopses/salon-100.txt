Salon 100, "Hello Uzbekistan" (September 2025), is a milestone salon about the group's exhibition and workshops in Uzbekistan, moderated in part by Annette Weintraub. Participating artists each present their piece and their experience, in alphabetical order: Cynthia Beth Rubin [[17:55|who works with plankton]], Gregory Little [[22:34|from Oberlin, Ohio]], Gregory Patrick Garvey [[27:02|who describes how the project began in 2023]], Lee Day [[31:07|with "Clipping Suburbia, Japan"]], Michael Woodruff [[35:54|from London, in his first exhibition printing the work]], Negin Ehtesabian and Patrick Lichty [[41:18|with "The House on the Water"]], Roz Dimon [[46:07|on how she came to Uzbekistan through Rubin]] and Stephen Paré [[51:18|on spreading the word through social media]]. The salon ends with [[1:36:11|an invitation to viewers of the recording to join the group]].
