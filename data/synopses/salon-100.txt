Salon 100, "Hello Uzbekistan" (September 2025), is a milestone salon about the group's exhibition and workshops in Uzbekistan, moderated in part by Annette Weintraub. Participating artists each present their piece and their experience, in alphabetical order: Cynthia Beth Rubin [[18:03|who works with plankton]], Gregory Little [[22:45|from Oberlin, Ohio]], Gregory Patrick Garvey [[27:13|who describes how the project began in 2023]], Lee Day [[31:18|with "Clipping Suburbia, Japan"]], Michael Woodruff [[36:05|from London, in his first exhibition printing the work]], Negin Ehtesabian and Patrick Lichty [[41:28|with "The House on the Water"]], Roz Dimon [[46:15|on how she came to Uzbekistan through Rubin]] and Stephen Paré [[51:26|on spreading the word through social media]]. The salon ends with [[1:36:19|an invitation to viewers of the recording to join the group]].
