Salon 56, "Cultural Influences" (November 9, 2022), has artists present in short turns on the cultural sources of their work. Speakers include [[1:36|Verneda Lights]], [[9:01|Giovanna Sun]], [[18:22|Marlow Shami and Tommy Mintz]], Susan Detroy [[37:05|on aging and how culture shapes our understanding of it]], Michael Pierre Price [[45:00|on cultural identity and feeling like a stranger in a strange land]] and Colin Goldberg [[50:55|on his mixed Jewish and Japanese-American background]]. The discussion later turns to [[1:16:26|how the artist index helps curators]] and [[1:42:10|a personal view of wonder without religion]].
