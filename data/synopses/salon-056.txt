Salon 56, "Cultural Influences" (November 9, 2022), has artists present in short turns on the cultural sources of their work. Speakers include [[1:36|Verneda Lights]], [[9:01|Giovanna Sun]], [[18:22|Marlow Shami and Tommy Mintz]], [[37:05|Susan Detroy, on aging and how culture shapes our understanding of it]], [[45:00|Michael Pierre Price, on cultural identity and feeling like a stranger in a strange land]] and [[50:55|Colin Goldberg, on his mixed Jewish and Japanese-American background]]. The discussion later turns to [[1:16:26|how the artist index helps curators]] and [[1:42:10|a personal view of wonder without religion]].
