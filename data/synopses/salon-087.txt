Salon 87, "Wild and Newfangled Techspressionism" (October 3, 2024), coincides with the opening of a collaborative exhibition with the Museum of Wild and Newfangled Art (Mauna), whose co-founders Cari Ann Shim Sham and Joey Zaza curated it. Colin Goldberg [[0:02|opens the salon]], and the curators give [[3:08|a statement on the exhibition and the shared commitments of Mauna, Techspressionism and Loop Art Critique]]. Artists in the show then present, including [[21:15|a presenter from North Providence, Rhode Island]], [[43:43|an artist in lower Manhattan]], [[57:15|a piece about storytelling and pattern making]], [[1:11:43|a piece from 1995 made when the artist worked at the World Trade Center]], [[1:18:56|small imagined life forms]], [[1:34:13|"Quadramid," a pyramid built from 289 3D pixels]] and [[1:55:45|a generative work by Stefano Contiero on the Highlight platform]]. The salon closes with [[2:09:42|thanks to everyone who contributed]].
