In Interview 23, Helen A. Harrison, director of the Pollock-Krasner House and Study Center and advisor to the Techspressionism exhibition, interviews artist Suzanne Anker. They open with [[2:20|the hidden world within the artist]] and [[7:50|Rorschach tests]], then Anker screen-shares [[10:31|images of her work]], including [[19:36|a 3D Rorschach test that new technology made possible]], and discusses [[13:18|visual interference in information]] and [[18:34|a video of the brain in action set to music]]. Harrison and Anker consider [[24:15|whether people will still paint as long as paint is sold]], [[28:02|learning to use a new medium]] and [[29:19|Lee Krasner's destroyed and reused work]]. Anker talks about [[31:58|the hardest part of her work: deciding what to do next]] and [[34:22|her international shows, including one at the ZKM Museum in Karlsruhe]].
