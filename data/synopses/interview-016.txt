In Interview 16, Colin Goldberg talks with artist, musician and writer Paul D. Miller, known as DJ Spooky that Subliminal Kid. Miller describes [[7:19|his schooling]], reading widely and how [[7:43|college became a foundational moment]], and [[11:19|working as a musician, artist and writer after moving to New York]], including [[11:48|throwing parties in a club scene on the Lower East Side]]. They discuss [[13:39|how everything is now algorithmically driven, the subject of his current book]], the commercial work that supports artists and [[20:15|abstract expressionism in its post-war context]]. Miller screen-shares [[26:47|a walk through Quantopia and the early internet of 1969]], and the interview closes with [[33:46|pointers to his website and books]].
