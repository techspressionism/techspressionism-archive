Salon 9 (January 19, 2021), moderated by Davonte Bradley, features five artists. Roz Dimon [[3:58|introduces herself and her digital work]], Lucie Králíková [[16:55|shows her process as a student at the Academy of Fine Arts in Prague]], and Patrick Lichty [[24:13|presents from Winona State University]].
