Interview 4 is a short, informal webcast that Colin Goldberg recorded with technology artist and writer Joseph Nechvatal for the Everbeta Content Network around 2000. Nechvatal explains [[1:22|Viractuality]], a hybrid of actuality and virtuality, and [[2:20|the third space between the physical work of art and the digital]]. He talks about [[5:21|the creative freedom of a techno-nomadic life split between New York and Europe]], takes the camera on [[6:18|a walk through the Lower East Side]], mentions [[7:52|the three-year work he made for his PhD]] and [[11:18|gives a short slideshow of his work]].
