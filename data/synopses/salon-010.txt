Salon 10 (February 2, 2021), moderated by Davonte Bradley, marks ten meetings held every two weeks. Anne Morgan Spalter [[3:24|describes her long use of computers in art]] and [[10:47|an experiment in generating images algorithmically]]. Darcy Gerbarg [[15:27|takes the group through her virtual-reality worlds]] and shows [[38:13|how she paints with a brush in each hand]]. Sherry Karver [[42:25|presents her work from Oakland]], including [[49:58|"The Conversation," printed as dye sublimation on metal]]. Brandon Gellis, a University of Wyoming professor, [[54:09|presents his new-media practice]], and Bradley [[1:08:00|shows his digital drawing-tablet work]], including [[1:22:09|a piece called "Aspire"]]. The discussion covers [[1:29:13|platforms for showing work in virtual spaces]], [[1:43:42|the cost of building a custom gallery and the problems with Unity]] and [[1:58:52|NFT marketplaces such as Rarible]].
