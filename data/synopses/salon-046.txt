Salon 46, "Weird Media" (June 22, 2022), is moderated by Patrick Lichty and previews a "weird media" exhibition organized with the IDMA. Lichty describes [[13:24|the IDMA and its ties to Bowling Green and Columbia College Chicago]] and [[18:07|how the show approached the theme]]. Artists in the show present their work, including [[24:09|Victor Acevedo, with a 77-second video]], [[29:59|Renata Janiszewska, on a clone character in "Crying Pink"]], [[36:31|Colin Goldberg, on a laser-etched piece from his wireframe series]], [[41:35|Malavika Mandal Andrew]], [[46:54|Tommy Mintz, on very large prints]] and [[52:52|Uli Ap]]. Lichty then goes through [[1:05:16|the remaining artists in the show]].
