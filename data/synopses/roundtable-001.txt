Roundtable 1, "Curators in Conversation," is the first Techspressionist roundtable. Colin Goldberg moderates a discussion between Helen A. Harrison, director of the Pollock-Krasner House and Study Center, and Christiane Paul, curator of digital art at the Whitney Museum. Harrison recalls [[8:36|when Goldberg first approached her about Techspressionism in 2011]], and Paul opens with [[12:17|a presentation on digital art]]. The conversation covers [[26:39|the distinction between a tool and a medium]], [[28:30|why Pollock is not accused of sucking the oxygen out of paint]] and [[30:41|remediation, using the Mona Lisa as an example]]. Goldberg tells [[31:35|the origin story of the term]], and they discuss [[36:19|changing attitudes to reproductive technologies]], [[43:39|how commercialization widens the market and the audience]], [[46:38|NFT collectors and the understanding of art]] and [[47:40|artists' royalties on secondary sales]].
