Salon 86, "Hello Brooklyn" (September 5, 2024), is moderated by Renata Janiszewska and presents artists from the Hello Brooklyn exhibition curated by Tommy Mintz. Speakers include [[12:04|Roz Dimon, from Shelter Island]], [[22:44|Cari Ann Shim Sham, co-founder of the Mauna Museum]], [[29:30|Tommy Mintz]], [[59:11|Joey Zaza]], [[1:04:55|Michael Pierre Price]], [[1:16:36|Renata Janiszewska, on the augmented-reality component]], [[1:27:24|Deann Stein Hasinoff, with "Just a Mom"]], [[1:31:34|Patrick Lichty]] and [[1:39:23|Verneda Lights, with an AI piece titled "Wise as the Serpent and Harmless as the Dove"]].
