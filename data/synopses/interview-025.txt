In Interview 25, Colin Goldberg talks with artist and physician Verneda Lights of Port Royal, South Carolina. She describes [[0:28|her Charleston birth and Port Royal upbringing]] and [[2:10|what being Gullah means, with its African retentions in music and culture]], and recalls [[11:48|deciding as a small child to become a doctor]] and [[14:01|the artistic friends she made along the way]]. She explains [[18:14|teaching herself to use a computer]] and [[19:50|how she builds digital pieces from colors, random placements, shapes and lines]], then screen-shares her work, including [[24:21|Pearl of Great Price, part of the Beauty for Ashes 9/11 art memorial]]. She discusses [[34:46|what Techspressionism means to her]], [[40:20|the written history and word origins of Gullah]] and [[46:51|a portrait series about a man's life]], and gives her view on [[1:00:02|the future of art]].
