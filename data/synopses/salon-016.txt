Salon 16 (April 27, 2021), moderated by Colin Goldberg, features three artists. Randi Matushevitz [[1:00|presents for the first time, describing herself as originally a painter]]. Gary Hopkins [[22:18|describes painting since childhood and being pushed toward science and math]], and shows how [[31:24|grid patterns of buildings and lakes]] enter his work, including [[40:28|a piece called "Bright Horns"]]. Roz Dimon [[57:41|presents last]]. The discussion covers [[1:07:22|why we make art]], [[1:26:14|plans for an exhibition reception]] and [[1:54:35|the divide between commercial art and fine art]].
