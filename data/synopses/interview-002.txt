In Interview 2, Colin Goldberg talks with digital artist and academic Anne Spalter. She recalls [[1:28|growing up in Brookline, Massachusetts]] and the traditional and mathematical influences behind her work, then screen-shares [[10:57|AI-assisted pieces that combine airplane photographs with Hans Hofmann paintings]] and explains [[12:45|how the algorithm iterates on its inputs]]. She describes [[14:52|her early opposition to using computers in art]] and [[19:04|the pushback she got from visiting critics]], and notes that [[24:34|a year of all-oil paintings sold out at an art fair]]. The conversation moves to [[26:10|selling crypto art on SuperRare]], [[33:34|how NFTs relate to unique and editioned work]], generative art and [[40:34|the early computer-art pioneers she collects]], including Hockney's experiments with technology. She talks about [[44:29|what draws her to Techspressionism]] and shows [[48:26|her exhibition conceived during the pandemic]].
