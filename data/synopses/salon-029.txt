Salon 29 (October 26, 2021) is the opening of the Techspressionism 21 virtual exhibition, which includes about 93 artists from around the world, with Davonte Bradley guiding a tour. Colin Goldberg [[2:44|explains how the term first appeared in print in the exhibition catalog, with an essay by Helen Harrison]], and Patrick Lichty [[15:15|reads a statement on art and its age]]. Artists then talk about their pieces, including [[25:05|Nina Yankowitz]], [[28:08|Verneda Lights]], [[31:18|Chalda Maloff]], [[35:49|Holly Gordon]], [[38:45|Uli Ap]], [[41:37|Karen LaFleur]], [[44:26|Sue Beyer]], [[47:11|Steve Miller]], [[49:49|Cynthia Beth Rubin]], [[54:10|Deann Stein Hasinoff]], [[56:58|Cynthia DiDonato]], [[1:00:32|Roz Dimon]], [[1:04:24|Jan Swinburne]], [[1:10:03|Victor Acevedo]], [[1:12:49|Malavika Mandal Andrew]], [[1:15:29|Michael Pierre Price]], [[1:18:13|Renata Janiszewska]], [[1:20:43|Diana de Avila]], [[1:24:11|Clive Holden]], [[1:30:30|Goldberg]], [[1:33:45|Lee Musgrave]], [[1:38:46|Alan Kinnard]], [[1:41:38|ScoJo]], [[1:44:24|Lee Schnaiberg]], [[1:49:08|Negin Ehtesabian]], [[1:57:40|Sean Mick]], [[2:01:28|Lucie Králíková]], [[2:04:27|Caro Ramonde]] and [[2:10:01|Cheryl Audet-Lavoie]]. Goldberg also explains [[2:07:03|how the group finds artists through the Techspressionism hashtag on Instagram]].
